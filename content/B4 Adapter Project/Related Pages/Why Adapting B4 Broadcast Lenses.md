## Advantages
- True parfocal design
- Massive zoom ranges
- From pretty wide to very tight focal length
- Relatively fast apertures
- Pretty small and light considering the range
- Built-in rocker for slow and controlled zooms

## What to be aware of
- Design to covers small sensor 
- Still results in a relatively large and heavy rig overall
- For tight shot, a tripod is almost mandatory
- Handheld can be cumbersome (the lens is designed for shoulder rigs)
