## Definition

Distance from the lens mount to the camera sensor.

## Common flange distances

| **Mount**   | **Flange (mm)** | **Sensor** | **Sensor size (mm)** |
| ----------- | --------------- | ---------- | -------------------- |

| Canon EOS-M | 18              | APSC       | 22.2x14.8            |
